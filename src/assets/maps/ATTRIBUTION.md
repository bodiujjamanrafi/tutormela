# Bangladesh district geometry

Source: geoBoundaries gbOpen BGD ADM2, boundary ID `BGD-ADM2-16705992`, representing 2020 boundaries, 64 districts.

Original boundary sources: Bangladesh Bureau of Statistics (BBS), OCHA ROAP.
License: Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Intergovernmental Organisations (CC BY 3.0 IGO), as declared by geoBoundaries.

- Metadata: https://www.geoboundaries.org/api/current/gbOpen/BGD/ADM2/
- Original source: https://data.humdata.org/dataset/administrative-boundaries-of-bangladesh-as-of-2015
- Retrieved geometry: https://media.githubusercontent.com/media/wmgeolab/geoBoundaries/9469f09/releaseData/gbOpen/BGD/ADM2/geoBoundaries-BGD-ADM2_simplified.geojson
- Attribution guidance: https://www.geoboundaries.org/index.html

Adaptations: projected to SVG coordinates, simplified for display, updated English district spellings to match the tutor catalog, label positioning, relief rendering, and interactive selection. Intended for tutor discovery, not surveying or legal boundary determination.

Fonts are locally hosted: Public Sans (U.S. Web Design System) and Source Serif 4 (Adobe), both under the SIL Open Font License. No font tracking or third-party runtime requests.

Bengali district spellings and district-to-division membership were checked against Bangladesh's National Portal: https://bangladesh.gov.bd/views/district-list/ . Each division miniature is composed from its member districts' original boundary paths. Label anchors are interior points; complete rendered names are fitted to their own polygon and clipped to that same polygon. No outside leader labels are used.

Additional locally hosted fonts: IBM Plex Sans and Noto Serif Bengali, under the SIL Open Font License. License files are retained under `public/fonts/`.
